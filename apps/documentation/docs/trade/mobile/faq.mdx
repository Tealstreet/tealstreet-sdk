## Reporting a problem

The app doesn't have a "report a bug" button yet. Report problems to us directly:

- **Discord:** join [our Discord server](https://teal.link/discord) and post in the **issues** channel. This is the
  fastest way to reach the team. See the [Discord guide](/docs/support/discord) if you're new to Discord.
- **Email:** [support@tealstreet.io](mailto:support@tealstreet.io).

Help us fix it quickly by including:

- **iPhone or Android**, and the **app version** shown at the bottom of the side panel.
- **What you did and what happened**: the exchange, the market, and the exact text of any error message.
- **A screenshot or screen recording**, if you can. **Check it doesn't show an API key, secret or QR code** before you
  send it.

If the app shows an **Application Error** screen, tap **📋 Copy Stack Trace & Error Details** and paste the result
into your report. Read it through before you send it, and remove anything that looks like a key or secret.

:::danger Never share your keys
We will never ask for your API secret, your `accounts.json` file, your cloud sync password or a picture of an account
QR code. Anyone who asks for them is not from Tealstreet.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need new API keys for the phone?

No. Bring your existing keys across from the web app (see
[Connecting your exchange accounts](./connect-accounts.mdx)). A key works on any number of devices.

### Is it the same Tealstreet account as the web app?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google or email login you use on the web.

### I imported an account and it says I'm signed out on this phone

See ["you are signed out on this phone"](./connect-accounts.mdx#your-account-export-is-fine-but-you-are-signed-out-on-this-phone-sign-in-again-then-import).
In short: tap **Sign In** on the alert (or the sign-in icon at the **top-right**), then import again. If you were
already signed in, tap the Tealstreet logo at the **top-left**, tap **Log Out**, then sign in again from the
top-right.

### How do I sign out, or switch to another Tealstreet login?

Tap the Tealstreet logo at the **top-left**, then **Log Out**. After the app restarts, tap the **sign-in icon** at the
**top-right**. The sign-in page lets you choose a different account.

### We said a fix is out, but I don't see it

Tap **Check for Updates** in the side panel and choose **Update now**. If the app says the update is already
downloaded, fully close Tealstreet (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. Some fixes need a new
build, which comes through TestFlight (iOS) or App Tester (Android). See
[App updates](./getting-started.mdx#4-app-updates).

### Do my chasers and TWAPs keep running when I close the app?

Yes, for up to 48 hours after your last device disconnects. See [Cloud jobs](./feature-tour.mdx#cloud-jobs).

### Does the app ask me to confirm orders?

No. The order button, **Close** and **✕ All** act on the first tap. See
[Placing your first trade](./first-trade.mdx).

### My exchange isn't available on mobile

Not every exchange is supported on mobile yet. If you see "_Exchange_ isn't available on mobile yet.", that account
stays on the web for now. Your other accounts work normally on the phone.

### My TestFlight or Firebase invite never arrived

Check your spam folder for emails from **App Store Connect**, **TestFlight** or **Firebase App Distribution**. iOS
invites expire if they're not accepted within about three days. If yours has expired or never arrived, contact us
and we'll send it again. Make sure we have your **Apple ID** email for iOS.

### Can I still use Tealstreet in my phone's browser?

Yes. See [Using Tealstreet in your phone's browser](/docs/trade/mobile#using-tealstreet-in-your-phones-browser).
