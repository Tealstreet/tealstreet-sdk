The app has three areas you'll use all the time:

- the **tab bar** along the bottom;
- the **top bar**, which holds the side-panel button, the market selector and your account, with the **account bar**
  under it;
- the **side panel**, behind the Tealstreet logo at the top-left.

## The top bar

| Where         | What it does                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Top-left**  | The Tealstreet logo opens the [side panel](#the-side-panel).                                               |
| **Middle**    | The current symbol. Tap it to search for and switch markets. On the **Market** tab this is a search field. |
| **Top-right** | Your active exchange account, shown as its logo in a circle (an empty circle until you add an account). Tap it to open or close the account bar. If you're signed out, this is the **sign-in icon**. |

The **account bar** under the top bar is open when the app starts. It has a pill for each account (tap one to switch). On the
**Trade** tab it also holds the chart selector and the **bell** for price alerts. While you have no accounts it shows
an **Add an API key** pill.

## The tab bar

### Trade

Where you place and manage trades. From top to bottom: the chart, a margin strip, the order type selector, the order
form with the order book beside it, and the **Positions**, **Orders** and **Margin** tabs. The **Margin** tab is where
you set leverage. See
[Placing your first trade](./first-trade.mdx).

### Market

A list of markets for the active exchange, with filters for **All Markets**, **Pinned**, **Perp**, **Futures** and
**Spot**. Tap a market to open its chart screen, with a stats header and an order drawer for trading from there. The
top bar and account bar stay in place.

:::info Screenshot needed
**SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER: `tab-market`**: the Market tab on **All Markets**.
:::

### Pro

Being rebuilt. It currently shows **Coming soon**. Use the **Trade** tab in the meantime.

### Portfolio

An overview of your account: **Adjusted Balance**, **Holdings**, **Margin** (**Available** and **Used**),
**Exposure** (**Long** and **Short**), and your **Positions** and **Orders** across symbols.

:::info Screenshot needed

### News

Tealstreet's live news feed, with **Everything** and **Traders** tabs (plus **Custom** if you've set up your own
list). Feed and filter options are under **Settings → News Trading**.

## The side panel

Tap the Tealstreet logo at the top-left. Your name or email is at the top, and the app version is at the bottom.

| Item                  | What it's for                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settings**          | All app settings (below).                                                                       |
| **API keys**          | Straight to **Settings → Accounts**: add, edit or remove exchange accounts.                     |
| **Price alerts**      | Your price alerts. The number in brackets is how many are active.                               |
| **Order history**     | Past orders.                                                                                    |
| **Cloud jobs**        | Accounts, chasers and TWAPs running on Tealstreet's servers (below). The number in brackets is how many jobs are running. |
| **Check for Updates** | Look for an in-app update now. See [App updates](./getting-started.mdx#4-app-updates).             |
| **Log Out**           | Sign out of Tealstreet on this phone.                                                           |

## Price alerts

Get a push notification on your phone when a price crosses your level. Each alert fires once, then switches off.

1. Open **Price alerts** from the side panel, or tap the bell in the account bar on the **Trade** tab.
2. Under **New alert**, pick the exchange from the row at the top.
3. Type the symbol (the field's hint shows that exchange's symbol format), choose **Above** or **Below**, and enter
   the price.
4. Tap **Create alert**. It appears under **Active alerts**.

You can also tap **+** on the chart at a price, then the bell on the card, to start an alert at that price. Allow notifications for Tealstreet when your
phone asks. Without notification permission, alerts can't reach you.

## Cloud jobs

On mobile your exchange accounts run on Tealstreet's servers (**DELEGATED mode**). So your chasers and TWAPs keep
working after you close the app. In the app's own words:

> Tealstreet's server runs the accounts switched on on this device, with their chasers and TWAPs, while this app is
> closed. An account keeps running for up to 48 h after your last device disconnects, or less when it trades through
> a proxy. Then its jobs stop and its key is erased. Stopping a chaser cancels its working order; your other resting
> orders and positions stay.

The **Cloud jobs** screen lists each account and its running jobs:

- A **TWAP** can be paused (**Pause** / **Resume**) or stopped (**Stop**). A **chaser** can only be stopped.
- **Stop jobs** on an account stops that account's jobs.
- **Stop running in cloud** ends the account's cloud session from this device.
- **Stop all cloud jobs** appears when at least one account has jobs running.

**Stop running in cloud** only affects this device. If another of your devices is connected to it, the server keeps it running for that device. Resting orders
and open positions stay on the exchange either way.

## Settings

| Group        | Sections                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Accounts** | **Accounts**: API keys, connected exchanges and **Cloud sync**. **Exchange**: per-exchange overrides.             |
| **Trading**  | **General**: defaults shared with the desktop app. **Place Order**: order form layout. **Chart**: chart engine, candles and indicators. **News Trading**: feeds, filters and quick orders. **Notifications**: push notifications to this phone. |
| **App**      | **Mobile**: density, haptics and this device. **Performance**: render rate and battery. **Languages**: interface language. |
| **Advanced** | **Developer**: diagnostics and debug tooling.                                                                     |

The **General** section holds trading defaults that are shared with the desktop app.
