This walkthrough assumes you're signed in and have [connected an exchange account](./connect-accounts).

:::warning Orders are sent immediately
The mobile app doesn't ask "are you sure?" before sending an order or closing a position. The order button,
**Close** and **✕ All** all act on the first tap. Start with a small size until you're used to the controls.
:::

## Pick the account and market

- **Account:** under the top bar there's an account bar with a pill for each of your accounts. It's open when the app
  starts. Tap the account you want. The circle at the **top-right** shows the active account and opens or closes that
  bar.
- **Market:** tap the **symbol in the middle of the top bar** and search for the market, for example `BTCUSDT`.

## How the Trade tab is laid out

From top to bottom: the chart, a margin strip, the **order type selector**, the **order form** with the order book
beside it, and then the **Positions**, **Orders** and **Margin** tabs.

The order type selector switches between:

| Type       | What it does                                                                                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Limit**  | Rests on the order book at your price until it fills or you cancel it.                           |
| **Market** | Fills straight away at the best available price.                                                 |
| **Quick**  | Rows of one-tap buy and sell orders a few ticks from the current price (see [Quick](#quick)).    |
| **Scaled** | Spreads several limit orders between an **Upper Price** and a **Lower Price**.                   |
| **TP/SL**  | A stop order that protects an open position.                                                     |

Above the form are the **Buy** and **Sell** pills. **They only choose the side**; they don't send anything. The
order is sent by the big **order button** under the form. It's labelled **Buy** or **Sell** to match the side you
picked, and shows the size and leverage. Under the button, an info card shows **Adjusted Balance**,
**Available Margin** and the initial margin the order will use.

**Size:** type the size into the **Size** field. The dollar buttons (for example **$100**) **add** that much
notional to the current size each time you tap them, and **CC** clears the size.

### If you see "Set leverage to trade"

Some exchanges have no leverage setting for a market until you choose one. The form then shows a
**Set leverage to trade** button. Tap it and choose a leverage on the slider. (The slider on the **Margin** tab does
the same.) Tealstreet sends the leverage to the exchange as soon as you stop moving the slider, and the order button
unlocks.

## Place a market order

1. Choose **Market** in the order type selector.
2. Tap the **Buy** pill to go long or the **Sell** pill to go short.
3. Enter a **Size**.
4. Tap the **order button** under the form.

A notice confirms the order, and the new position appears in the **Positions** tab.

## Place a limit order

1. Choose **Limit**.
2. Tap the **Buy** or **Sell** pill.
3. Enter a **Price**. Or turn on **BBO** to have the price follow the best bid (for a buy) or best ask (for a sell).
4. Enter a **Size**.
5. Optional:
    - **Post**: post-only. The order is only accepted if it rests on the book, so it never fills as a taker.
    - **Reduce**: reduce-only. The order can only shrink your position, never open or flip it.
6. Tap the **order button**.

The order appears in the **Orders** tab until it fills.

## Quick

**Quick** swaps the Buy/Sell pills for an **Offer** / **Take** switch, and shows rows of **Buy** and **Sell** buttons
at set tick distances from the price. Each tap sends one order straight away.

- **Offer** prices from your side of the book, so the order rests and waits to be filled.
- **Take** prices across the spread, so the order fills straight away.

## Take profit and stop loss

- **Stop loss:** choose **TP/SL**, pick the side that closes your position (a **Sell** stop protects a long, a
  **Buy** stop protects a short), set the trigger price, and tap the order button (labelled **Stop**).
  TP/SL orders are always reduce-only: **Reduce** is locked on. The **Full Stop** switch decides how much closes:
    - **Full Stop** on: the whole position closes when the stop triggers.
    - **Full Stop** off: only the **Size** you enter closes.
- **Take profit:** place a **Limit** order on the other side of your position with **Reduce** turned on. For a long,
  that's a sell limit above your entry. Reduce-only makes sure it can only close the position.

Your stops and take-profit orders are listed in the **Orders** tab. Use its filter to show **Stops**.

## Watch your positions

The **Positions** tab lists your open positions. **Swipe a position row to the left** to show its buttons. (Tapping
the row opens its details instead.)

- **Close**: closes the whole position at market straight away (a reduce-only market order).
- **BE**: places a **breakeven** stop loss at your entry price for the whole position.
- **Actions**: opens **Quick Actions**:
    - **Market** and **Limit** tabs, plus **Chase** where available: buttons to add to or reduce the position by a
      percentage, and **Reverse** to flip it.
    - **Close** tab: close using **Limit** or **Market** (or **Chase**), or spread over time with **TWAP 1m** /
      **TWAP 5m**.

**Chase** only appears when chasers are turned on in settings (they are by default) and your exchange supports them.

**✕ All (N)** at the top of the tab closes **every** open position at market, with no confirmation.

## Manage open orders

The **Orders** tab lists resting orders and stops. Its filter at the top chooses which orders you see (for example
all orders, limit/market only, or stops).

- **Cancel one order:** swipe the row left and tap **Cancel**. Or tap the row to open its details, where you can
  **Cancel** or **Edit** it.
- **Cancel several:** use the buttons at the top: **✕ Buys**, **✕ Sells**, **✕ _Symbol_** or **✕ All**. These only
  cancel orders that **match the current filter**. If the filter is on **Stops**, **✕ All** cancels only your stops.
- **Cancel from the order book:** tap (or long-press) a price level where you have orders. Tealstreet asks
  **Cancel Orders** at that price before cancelling.

## Charts

- The chart is at the top of the **Trade** tab. Use the chart's top bar to change the timeframe and add indicators.
- Tap **+** on the chart at a price to open a small order card at that price. Its type menu offers **LIMIT**,
  **MARKET** and **SL** (stop loss). The bell on the card sets a price alert at that price instead.
- For a bigger chart with its own order drawer, open the **Market** tab and tap any market.
- The **bell** in the account bar opens **Price alerts**. If you can't see it, tap the circle at the top-right to
  open the account bar.
