This page takes you from "I'd like the app" to signed in. When you're done, continue with
[Connecting your exchange accounts](./connect-accounts).

## 1. Request an invite

The app is invite-only during early access. Request an invite from the Tealstreet web app:

1. Open Tealstreet on your computer and sign in.
2. Open **Updates** in the header and switch to the **Mobile** tab.
3. Under **Mobile early access**, click **Request invite**.
4. Pick your **Platform**:
    - **iOS**: enter your **Apple ID Email**. TestFlight sends the invite to your Apple ID, which may not be the
      email you use for Tealstreet.
    - **Android**: enter the **Email** that should get the invite.
5. Click **Send request**.

The team adds each invite by hand, so the invite can take a little while to arrive. If you have already been
invited directly (for example by the team in Discord or by email), skip this step.

## 2. Install the app

### iPhone and iPad (TestFlight)

iOS invites come in **two emails from Apple**, and you need both:

1. **An App Store Connect invitation.** Accept it first. It adds you to Tealstreet's testing team. Apple drops an
   unaccepted invitation after about three days, so don't leave it in your inbox. If it expires, ask us for a new one.
2. **A TestFlight invitation**, which arrives after you accept the first one.

Then:

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Open the TestFlight invitation email on your iPhone and tap the link. Or open TestFlight and redeem the code from
   the email.
3. Tap **Install** next to **Tealstreet**.

TestFlight installs new builds as they come out. Keep automatic updates on in TestFlight, or open TestFlight now
and then to update.

:::tip Check your spam folder
Apple's invitation emails often end up in spam or promotions. Search your inbox for "TestFlight" and
"App Store Connect".
:::

### Android (Firebase App Distribution)

Android builds come through Firebase App Distribution, not the Play Store:

1. Open the invitation email from **Firebase App Distribution** on your Android phone and accept it.
2. Follow the email's link to install **Firebase App Tester**. App Tester isn't in the Play Store, so Android asks
   you to allow installs from this source. Allow it for the browser or app you're installing from.
3. Open App Tester, find **Tealstreet**, and tap **Download**, then install it.

App Tester tells you when a new build is available. Each Firebase build expires about 150 days after release, so
install the newer build when App Tester offers one.

## 3. Sign in

The app opens on the **Trade** tab even before you sign in, with live public market data. Your accounts, balances
and orders need you to sign in with your Tealstreet account. Use the same login as the web app.

1. Tap the **sign-in icon** at the **top-right** of the screen. It's a small arrow-into-a-box icon.
2. Tealstreet's sign-in page opens. Choose **Google**, or sign in with your **email** and password. Use the same
   method you use on the web.
3. When the sign-in page closes, you're back in the app. The top-right icon becomes a circle. Once you've connected
   an exchange account, it shows that exchange's logo. Until then it's empty, and the Trade screen shows
   **No exchange account** with an **Add an API key** button.

### Signing out, and signing back in

- **Sign out:** tap the **Tealstreet logo** at the **top-left** to open the side panel, then tap **Log Out** (the
  red item at the bottom). The app restarts, signed out.
- **Sign back in:** tap the **sign-in icon** at the **top-right** again.

If the app ever says **"Your session has expired. Please log out and log in again."**, tap **Log Out**, wait for the
app to restart, then tap the top-right sign-in icon.

## 4. App updates

There are two kinds of update:

- **New builds** (a new TestFlight or Firebase build). Install these through TestFlight or App Tester as above.
- **In-app updates.** Most fixes are delivered straight to the app, without a new build.

The app checks for in-app updates when you open it and when you come back to it (at most every 15 minutes). A check
only looks: nothing is downloaded until you choose to. When an update is available, the app shows a full-screen
**Update available** message:

- **Update now** downloads the update and restarts Tealstreet straight away.
- **Later · 3 remaining** postpones it, before anything is downloaded. The app asks again when you come back after
  four hours. You can postpone the same update three times. After that it's required the next time you open the app.

You can also check yourself at any time with **Check for Updates** in the side panel.

### When an update loads on the next open

Sometimes **Update now** can't finish in one go. If the download is slow, the app lets you carry on rather than
keeping you waiting. If the download completes without the restart, you'll see **"The update is downloaded. Restart
now, or it will apply the next time you reopen Tealstreet."** Choose **Restart now**, or **Continue to app** and
keep trading.

A downloaded update loads the **next time the app starts fresh**: fully close Tealstreet (swipe it away in the app
switcher) and open it again. Switching to another app and back isn't enough.

So if we tell you a fix is out and you don't see it, tap **Check for Updates** in the side panel and choose
**Update now**. If you've already downloaded it, fully close and reopen the app.
