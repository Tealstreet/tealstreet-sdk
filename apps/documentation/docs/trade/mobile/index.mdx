Tealstreet has a native app for iPhone, iPad and Android. It uses the same Tealstreet account as the web app, and you
bring your exchange API keys across from the web in a few taps. Your positions and orders are on the exchange, so
you see the same ones on every device.

The app is in **early access**. iOS builds come through Apple's **TestFlight**, and Android builds come through
**Firebase App Distribution**. Neither is in the App Store or Play Store yet.

## How the app runs your accounts

On mobile, your exchange accounts run on Tealstreet's servers (Tealstreet calls this **DELEGATED mode**). As
**Settings → Accounts** puts it: _"Your accounts run on Tealstreet's servers. If an account can't connect, it stays
offline until it can."_

In practice this means the app does not have to stay open for your chasers and other algos to keep working. See
[Cloud jobs](./feature-tour.mdx#cloud-jobs) for what keeps running, and for how to stop it.

## Using Tealstreet in your phone's browser

You can still open the Tealstreet web app in your phone's browser. It is the full desktop layout scaled down, so
the native app is the better experience on a phone. If you do use the browser, the **Add to Home Screen** option
hides the browser's own toolbars and gives the page more room.
