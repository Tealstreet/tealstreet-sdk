The **Aggr modules** are mini-browser panels that embed [Aggr](https://charts.aggr.trade/) dashboards directly in Tealstreet. Two variants are available:

- **Aggr (Ticker)**: follows your active trading symbol (dynamic)
- **Aggr (BTC)**: always shows BTC markets (static)

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={Aggr2} alt="Aggr 2" style={{ width: 350 }} />
</div>

Aggr is an excellent way to keep your finger on the pulse of the market with its extremely low timeframes and tick chart support.

:::note
Aggr module is **not enabled by default** and needs to be manually activated from your module tile selector.
:::

---

### Enabling Aggr (Ticker Trades)

- Add the **Aggr (Ticker)** module from the module selector.
- **Dynamic Symbol**: Automatically follows your active trading symbol.
- **URL**: Loads from `https://aggr2.tealstreet.io/`.
- **Use Case**: Keep Aggr in sync with what you’re trading.

Covered markets (as embedded):

- Binance Futures
- Bybit
- Binance Spot
- Coinbase (USD and USDT pairs)

### Enabling Aggr (BTC)

- Add the **Aggr (BTC)** module from the module selector.
- **Static Symbol**: Always shows BTC regardless of your active symbol.
- **URL**: Loads from `https://aggr.tealstreet.io/`.
- **Use Case**: Keep a constant BTC Aggr view alongside your other modules.

---

### Enabling the Aggr modules

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={Aggr} alt="Aggr 1" style={{ width: 600 }} />
</div>

- You can enable either **Aggr (Ticker)** or **Aggr (BTC)** under the Modules tab.

:::tip
Remember to unlock your layout to be able to move the module around.
:::

:::warning
These modules are not included in the default layout and must be manually enabled before they appear in your trading interface.
:::

---

### Using the Aggr modules

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={Aggr3} alt="Aggr 3" style={{ width: 700 }} />
</div>

- Once activated and positioned how you like, click the "**...**" button in the lower right-hand corner inside Aggr to open Aggr’s own options.

:::note
**Behavior recap**

- **Aggr (Ticker)** mirrors your active symbol.
- **Aggr (BTC)** is always BTC.
  Both are iframe embeds and work in web and desktop layouts.
  :::
