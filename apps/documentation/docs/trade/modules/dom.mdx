---

The **DOM (Depth of Market)** module shows a ladder view of the orderbook with prices, size columns, and optional imbalance highlighting. It is optimized for fast reading and quick clicks directly on price levels.

::::caution
Some exchanges may throttle rapid clicks; use with care when placing frequent updates.
::::

---

### Key Features

- **Ladder View**: Prices centered around the mark/last, with bids/asks stacked vertically.
- **Size Columns**: Shows per-level size; can display cumulative or individual sizes depending on mode.
- **Imbalance Highlighting**: Optional shading to reveal skew between bid/ask depth.
- **Grouping/Precision**: Adjust price grouping to fit your symbol and zoom level.
- **Click to Stage**: Click a price level to stage an order price in the Place Order module (when supported by your exchange/account).

---

### Settings

- **Layout & Mode**: Choose stacked vs split, and cumulative vs individual sizes (matches Orderbook settings).
- **Grouping/Precision**: Set tick aggregation (price step) for the ladder.
- **Imbalance Display**: Toggle showing bid/ask imbalance for quick skew detection.
- **Auto-Center**: Keep the ladder centered on the mid/last price (if available).

---

### Usage Tips

- For thin books, lower the grouping to see more granular levels; for volatile pairs, widen grouping to reduce noise.
- Use imbalance shading to spot one-sided depth before placing orders.
- Combine with the Place Order module: click a DOM level, then adjust size/post/reduce before submitting.
