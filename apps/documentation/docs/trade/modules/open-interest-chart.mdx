The **Open Interest Chart module** opens a preconfigured [Basic Chart module](/docs/trade/modules/basic-chart) with open-interest data.

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={OpenInterestChart} alt="Open Interest Chart" style={{ width: 600 }} />
</div>

## Activating the Open Interest Chart module

- Open **Trade Settings**.
- Go to the **Modules** tab.
- Enable **Open Interest Chart**.

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={OpenInterestChartSettings} alt="Open Interest Chart Module" style={{ width: 350 }} />
</div>

Once activated, you can resize and reposition it like other modules.

:::note
Data source and symbol coverage depend on your current market/exchange context.
:::
