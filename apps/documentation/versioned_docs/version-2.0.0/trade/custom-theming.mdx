A new feature with Tealstreet 2.0 is the ability to customize almost any of colors you can see on the terminal.

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={ThemingButton} alt="ThemingButton" style={{ width: 750 }} />
</div>

- Click the palette button near the top right of your terminal to open the theming menu.

---

### Customizing your theme

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={ThemeSettings} alt="ThemeSettings" style={{ width: 300 }} />
</div>

- Remember to save after you make your changes. You can also export if you wish to share the layout with friends or submit them as a community present in our **[Discord](/docs/support/contact-us)**.

:::tip
Hit the reset button to return the theme to its default settings.
:::

---

### Theme Presets

<div class="text--center">
    <img src={ThemePresetDropdown} alt="ThemePresetDropdown" style={{ width: 600 }} />
</div>

- Use the slider-bar to switch between the presets created by the TS dev team at the top, or the community created presets lower down.

:::note
Like your own theme and want to share it? Contact us on our **[Discord](/docs/support/contact-us)** and we can add it to the list!
:::
