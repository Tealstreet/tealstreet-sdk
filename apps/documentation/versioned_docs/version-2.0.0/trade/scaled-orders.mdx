---

A Scaled order is an algorithmic tool that enables advanced users to automatically create multiple limit orders across a user-determined price range. This tool also provides control over the diversity and distribution of orders within the price range.

With a scaled buy, orders at lower limits are triggered in sequence as the market price drops; a scaled sell works in reverse, with orders at higher limits getting filled in turn as the market price increases.

:::tip
Scaled orders help avoid the market impact of buying or selling a large number of contracts in a single transaction. They can also be used to improve your average entry or exit price.
:::

The Tealstreet Scaled Order is a very powerful tool that you can use to spread your buy and sell orders any ways you like.


### How to Place a Scaled Order

:::danger
Due to the complexities of rounding, please note that scaled orders may submit a Size / Quantity with a ~5% margin of error.
:::

1. In the scaled order window set your **Upper** and **Lower** price.

2. Input the amount you want to trade and number of orders.

3. Select if you want to place buy or sell orders.

4. Input your preferred amount and price variance percentage (or leave it blank).

5. From the Price Distribution Chart choose how your order is going to be distributed.

6. Use the preview button to preview your order.

:::tip
You can use the reset button to reset the scaled order window to its default state.
:::

7. Use the submit button to submit your orders.

### Price Distribution

Tealstreet has a unique and powerful way of scaling your orders.

<div class="text--center">

![Price-Distribution](/img/trading/scaled-3.gif)

</div>

The distribution of **Price** and the **Amount** work in very similar ways. The line on the top (red/blue) adjusts the price and the line on the bottom (orange) adjusts the total amount. There are four options:

1. Flat: Set the orders and price in equal divisions across the order count.
2. Ascending: Set the orders and price in ascending order.
3. Descending: Set the orders and price in descending order.
4. Other: You can set the distribution uniquely to your liking by adjusting the nodes individually.
