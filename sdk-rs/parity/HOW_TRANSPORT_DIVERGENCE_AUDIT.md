Supported explanations account for 16/49 (32.7%). New finding rows comprise
30 justification gaps and two behavior differences. The already-open factory
row additionally receives a new annotation subcause but is counted once as a
prior finding, not as a newly finding-bearing row. Thus 33 notes change while
32 new finding rows are counted. Every original note remains a historical suffix.

The behavior differences are code-inspected only. Ordinary JSON-parser form
fallback drops a primitive __proto__ assignment in the source plain object;
the native JSON map retains an own field in transmitted arg.data. Bigint and
custom-parser modes are excluded from that contrast. A direct public onMessage
call after disposal also differs: the source plain-message entry skips decode
and dispatch, while native on_message re-enables time sync and invokes its
numeric callback/fallback. Existing native receive/monitor generation guards
prevent claiming a measured late-network race. No new execution or mutation
witness is claimed for either difference.

Cross-review kept concrete local ownership reasons supported: for example,
copying byte views simplifies native lifetimes, and host injection avoids two
credential providers. Existing constructor lazy-dial ownership and auth-lease
retry timing are already priced, so they are not new scheduling findings.
Conversely, incorrect MIXED/FORCED classification and a list of admitted costs
without a reason remain annotation findings. The private URL-summary consumer
chain terminates in console diagnostics; its bucket finding does not invent a
new unit-sensitive program consumer.

No status, target or mapping moved. Existing witness files were inspected,
not rerun. SHA-bound gate observations and explicit exclusions are recorded in
HOW_TRANSPORT_AUDIT_GATE_EXITS.md.
